TokenpoCket Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TokenpoCket collects, uses, stores, protects, and shares information when you use the app.

1. About TokenpoCket

TokenpoCket is a personal finance app designed to help users record transactions, manage accounts, track budgets, and review financial statistics. This policy applies to the TokenpoCket mobile application and related in-app services.

2. Information We Collect

TokenpoCket may process the following types of information:

  • Finance records you create: transaction amount, category, account name, date, note, tags, budget settings, and account balance information.
  • Local app preferences: settings required to remember app state, privacy confirmation status, cache status, and similar local preferences.
  • Optional file data: files you choose to import or export, such as CSV-compatible transaction data.
  • Device permissions: camera, microphone, storage, or media permissions may be requested only when a feature requires them and after system permission confirmation.
  • Network configuration data: the app may request remote startup configuration and may load web content when configured.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Save and display your personal finance records;
  • Provide budget tracking, account management, statistics, and charts;
  • Support import and export actions that you start;
  • Run optional permission-based features after you grant permission;
  • Load remote configuration or web content when enabled;
  • Maintain app stability, diagnose problems, and improve user experience.

4. Local Storage

TokenpoCket is designed around local finance management. Your finance records are primarily stored on your device. If you clear app data or uninstall the app, locally stored records may be removed unless you export them first.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: used for remote startup configuration and optional web content loading.
  • Camera: used only when a feature or web page requests camera access and you grant permission.
  • Microphone: used only for optional audio-related features and only after you grant permission.
  • Storage or media access: used only for file import/export or file-selection actions initiated by you.

TokenpoCket does not perform continuous background audio or video recording.

6. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be shared or made available to third parties only in limited situations, such as when you open external web content, select or upload a file, use a system file picker, or interact with a third-party service through WebView content. Third-party services process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by the app. Because no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, we recommend that you protect your device with system security options and export records only to TokenpoCketed locations.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You may:

  • Review this Privacy Policy at any time from the Profile page;
  • Revoke optional permissions in Android system settings;
  • Delete records in the app where supported;
  • Clear app data or uninstall the app to remove local app data;
  • Export your records before clearing or uninstalling the app.

9. Children's Privacy

TokenpoCket is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review and address the issue.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be made available in the app or on the related privacy policy page. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest version.

TrUth Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TrUth handles information when you manage homestay and rental turnover supplies in the app.

1. About TrUth

TrUth is a local-first operations app for homestays, short-term rentals, serviced apartments, and rental units. It helps users manage cleaning supplies, linen, repair pouches, guest amenities, turnover checks, and procurement tasks.

2. Information We Collect

TrUth may process the following types of information:

  • Turnover records you create: supply names, categories, unit or storage locations, quantities, review dates, kit plans, check schedules, purchase items, notes, and completion status.
  • Local app preferences: settings required to remember app state, privacy confirmation status, reminder status, cache status, and similar local preferences.
  • Optional file data: files you choose to import or export, such as local operations backups.
  • Device permissions: camera, microphone, storage, or media permissions may be requested only when a feature requires them and after system permission confirmation.
  • Network configuration data: the app may request remote startup configuration and may load web content when configured.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Save and display your supply, kit, inspection, and purchase records;
  • Support room turnover planning, linen refreshes, maintenance checks, and guest-ready setup;
  • Generate procurement suggestions from kit gaps and urgent supply reviews;
  • Support import and export actions that you start;
  • Run optional permission-based features after you grant permission;
  • Load remote configuration or web content when enabled;
  • Maintain app stability, diagnose problems, and improve user experience.

4. Local Storage

TrUth is designed around local operations management. Your turnover records are primarily stored on your device. If you clear app data or uninstall the app, locally stored records may be removed unless you export them first.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: used for remote startup configuration and optional web content loading.
  • Camera: used only when a feature or web page requests camera access and you grant permission.
  • Microphone: used only for optional audio-related features and only after you grant permission.
  • Storage or media access: used only for file import/export or file-selection actions initiated by you.

TrUth does not perform continuous background audio or video recording.

6. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be shared or made available to third parties only in limited situations, such as when you open external web content, select or upload a file, use a system file picker, or interact with a third-party service through WebView content. Third-party services process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by the app. Because no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, we recommend that you protect your device with system security options and export records only to trusted locations.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You may:

  • Review this Privacy Policy at any time from the Profile page;
  • Revoke optional permissions in Android system settings;
  • Delete records in the app where supported;
  • Clear app data or uninstall the app to remove local app data;
  • Export your records before clearing or uninstalling the app.

9. Children's Privacy

TrUth is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review and address the issue.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be made available in the app or on the related privacy policy page. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest version.
